Introduction

Before We Build Anything,
I Need to Understand You

Every brand is different. Before I develop a strategy, write a single line of copy, or make any recommendation, I take time to understand your business, your goals, and the people you are trying to reach.

That is why every project begins with a discovery process — and this document is the activation of that process. The questions that follow will shape everything — the strategy, the website copy, and the social media direction.

The more honest and detailed your answers, the stronger the outcome will be.

Frequently Asked Questions — Website Copy

What exactly is "website copy" and how is it different from content?
Website copy refers to the strategic text on each page of your website — headlines, descriptions, calls-to-action, and value propositions. It's different from blog content or articles because its primary job is to guide a visitor toward a specific action, like making an enquiry or understanding your services.
How many rounds of revision are included?
Each page of website copy includes two rounds of revision. The first draft is written from the brand strategy, then you provide feedback, and I refine. This process ensures the copy feels accurate and natural to your brand while remaining strategically effective.
Do I need to have a website designer before the copy is written?
No. In fact, it's best to have the copy written first. Good website design should be built around the messaging, not the other way round. The copy will come with clear structure and layout direction so your designer knows exactly what goes where.
Will the copy be SEO-friendly?
Yes. The copy will be written with search engine best practices in mind — clear headings, relevant language, and natural keyword usage. However, deep technical SEO (schema markup, site speed, etc.) falls outside this scope unless separately agreed.
What format will the website copy be delivered in?
You'll receive a structured document (Google Docs or PDF) with copy organized by page and section, including headlines, body copy, CTAs, and layout notes. This makes it easy for you or your designer to implement directly.
Can I add more pages later?
Absolutely. Additional pages (FAQ pages, landing pages, career pages, etc.) can be added at any time for a per-page rate. Since the brand voice and strategy are already established, additional pages are faster to produce.
